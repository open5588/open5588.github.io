Idea Title: ShelfSense AI — Smart Receipt-to-Fridge Expiry Copilot & Dynamic Neighborhood Meal-Kit Ledger

1. Problem and Target Users

Urban working families and dual-income households represent our primary target users. Globally, households generate sixty percent of the 1.05 billion tonnes of annual food waste, driven primarily by forgotten perishables pushed to the back of refrigerators, confusing date labels, and uncoordinated grocery purchasing between busy partners. Despite rising grocery inflation and strong environmental awareness, time-poor parents lack effortless tools to track what enters their kitchen and consume fresh produce, dairy, and meats before spoilage occurs.

2. Value Proposition

ShelfSense AI is a zero-hardware WhatsApp and iOS expiry copilot that transforms paper receipts, online grocery invoices, and quick refrigerator photos into an automated, living kitchen inventory in three seconds. Using multimodal optical character recognition and ambient temperature-humidity decay models, ShelfSense predicts dynamic real-world freshness curves for every perishable item. Forty-eight hours before high-risk ingredients spoil, the copilot pushes tailored fifteen-minute Rescue Recipes that combine expiring items already in the fridge. For unopened surplus items families cannot finish before travel or busy weeks, one tap publishes a verified Sealed-Surplus bundle to a hyper-local residential building micro-marketplace for instant neighbor pickup.

3. Business Model

ShelfSense operates a scalable B2B2C SaaS and affiliate grocery loop. Core receipt scanning, expiry alerts, and peer-to-peer neighbor sharing remain free for households to maximize neighborhood density. Revenue comes from three streams: licensing our white-label Expiry Copilot API at four cents per scan to regional supermarket loyalty apps seeking higher customer retention and timely private-label cross-sells; a $4.99 monthly Family Pro subscription featuring automated multi-partner shopping lists and macro-balanced meal prep; and a twelve percent micro-commission on discounted neighborhood surplus bundles.

4. Potential Impact

In pilot modeling across high-density urban apartment blocks, ShelfSense reduces household perishable food waste by forty-two percent within sixty days, saving the average four-person family $780 annually on groceries while avoiding 0.9 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent emissions per household each year. Scaling to one hundred thousand active urban households diverts over twenty-eight thousand tonnes of edible food from municipal landfills annually while strengthening neighborhood food resilience and circular community trust across participating cities.
