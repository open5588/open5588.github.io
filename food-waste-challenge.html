Part B: Business Idea — ShelfPulse

1. Idea Title

ShelfPulse: POS-Linked Household Perishable Tracker & Retailer Cashback Meal-Rescue Loop

2. Problem and Target Users

Households generate 60% of global food waste (630 million tonnes annually), primarily because busy working families and urban dual-income households forget perishable items hidden in refrigerators or misinterpret static "Best Before" labels. Existing food-waste apps fail within two weeks due to "manual logging fatigue"—users refuse to manually type or scan barcodes for thirty grocery items after every supermarket trip.

3. Value Proposition

ShelfPulse eliminates manual data entry by integrating directly with supermarket loyalty cards and digital e-receipts. Whenever a household shops, ShelfPulse automatically imports purchased perishables (dairy, leafy greens, meat, bakery) and assigns dynamic shelf-life countdowns adjusted for storage zone (fridge, crisper, freezer). Forty-eight hours before an item's peak freshness expires, ShelfPulse pushes a one-tap "Rescue Dinner Pairing" combining all expiring items in the user's fridge into a 15-minute recipe, or enables one-click neighbor sharing within the same apartment building. Users who consume or rescue at least 85% of their tracked perishables earn instant "Zero-Spoil Cashback" points redeemable on their next grocery bill, saving an average household €400–€550 annually.

4. Business Model

ShelfPulse operates a financially self-sustaining B2B2C revenue model where households use the core app for free: (a) Supermarket SaaS & Loyalty Retention Fee (€0.35/active household/month): Grocery chains license ShelfPulse to comply with EU/UK Scope 3 food-waste reduction mandates while increasing customer repeat visits via locked-in cashback credits. (b) CPG Complementary Recipe Sponsorship (CPA): Sustainable pantry brands pay performance fees when recommended as complementary non-perishable ingredients inside a user's expiring-food rescue recipe. (c) ShelfPulse Household Plus (€2.99/month optional tier): Unlocks automated family meal-prep scheduling and smart-fridge calendar syncing.

5. Potential Impact

By removing logging friction at the point of purchase and aligning household financial incentives with waste prevention, ShelfPulse can cut household perishable spoilage by 40–45%. Partnering with just three mid-sized regional supermarket chains (500,000 active households) prevents over 38,000 tonnes of edible food waste and 95,000 tonnes of CO2-equivalent emissions annually while scaling rapidly via existing retail POS APIs.
